# Pitch

ModelLedger is a digital passport for AI images. It does not guess where an image came from; it collects and verifies the evidence of how it was made and changed.

An image can pass through a generator, an AI editor and an ordinary export tool. ModelLedger records each signed export as a version, checks its exact binding, consults the actor and witness registry, and displays a graph of the evidence. Unsupported sections stay visibly unknown. Altered signatures and contradictory bindings are invalid, while absent evidence is simply unverifiable.

Blockchain supplies a public timeline, actor approvals, revocation and event anchors. It cannot make a false claim true. Independent corroboration and explicit policy determine how much trust an origin claim receives. Private prompts and seeds stay with the owner, represented by salted commitments rather than published text.
